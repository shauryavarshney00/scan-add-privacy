Privacy Policy for Scan Add Calculator

Effective Date: October 2026

Scan Add Calculator ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our mobile application handles user information and permissions.

1. Data Collection & Processing

Scan Add Calculator is designed with strict data minimization principles:

  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not collect, store, sell, or rent your personal information, name, email, phone number, contacts, or financial account details.
  • No Account Required: You do not need to register, log in, or provide any identity credentials to use the application.

2. Camera & Photo Storage Permissions

The app requests access to your device's Camera and Media Gallery exclusively to perform document scanning:

  • Camera Access: Used solely to allow you to take photos of paper ledger sheets, bills, or receipts for real-time mathematical OCR processing.
  • Gallery Access: Used solely to let you choose an existing ledger photo from your device.
  • Ephemeral Processing: Photos are analyzed strictly on-demand for OCR character isolation. We do NOT permanently store, archive, or upload your scanned ledger images to external advertising servers or third-party databases.

3. Network Security & Data Encryption

Whenever data is processed across encrypted channels, all network traffic is secured via industry-standard Transport Layer Security (HTTPS / TLS 1.3 encryption). We do not run background telemetry or sell any data to third-party ad networks.

4. Children's Privacy

Scan Add Calculator is a general-purpose accounting and productivity tool intended for shopkeepers, traders, and adults. We do not knowingly collect information from children under the age of 13.

5. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy periodically. Any modifications will be posted directly to this webpage with an updated effective date.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact developer support via your Google Play Console developer contact email.
